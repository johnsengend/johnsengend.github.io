Sustainable Transportation

Electric vehicles (EVs) represent the future of green transportation. Norway is a global leader in EV adoption, with more than 80% of new cars being electric. Oslo, in particular, is transforming into a model eco-city. This shift contributes to cleaner air, reduced carbon emissions, and a sustainable transport ecosystem.

Smart City Infrastructure

Smart cities use ICT and IoT to improve urban life through data-driven services. Norway is implementing cutting-edge smart infrastructure, ensuring sustainability, energy efficiency, and improved public services. From intelligent traffic systems to efficient energy use, smart infrastructure is reshaping urban landscapes.

The Future of Connectivity: 6G

6G will build upon the capabilities of 5G, enabling ultra-fast speeds, low latency, and massive connectivity. It will empower innovations like holographic communication, digital twins, and immersive virtual experiences. Norway is investing in R&D to be a leader in this next wave of telecom evolution.
